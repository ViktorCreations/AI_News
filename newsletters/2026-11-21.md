# AI News — Saturday, November 21, 2026

## Top Stories

### [An Anthropic mathematician claims, working with Claude, to have proven the Riemann hypothesis](https://siliconangle.com/2026/11/20/anthropic-researcher-claims-to-have-proven-riemann-hypothesis-using-claude/)
Levent Alpöge announced on his personal website on Friday that over about six weeks he used Claude to produce a proof, which he writes in a four-page statement is "one dawn across science of something far greater." SiliconANGLE notes Anthropic has not published an official announcement, and the [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c6pqr2g7w059o) stresses "if found to be correct" the proof would be among the most significant in history.

[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/20/ai-claimed-a-solution-to-the-167-year-old-riemann-hypothesis-and-a-human-did-too/) reports that mathematicians discussing it on the Zulip forum, including MIT's Andrew Sutherland, say they have confirmed that the formalized proof compiles in Lean, a proof-checking language, and that most appear confident it will hold. But the argument is not yet understood by humans: Alpöge released it without a human-readable explanation and has committed to producing one, and it will be a while before the community has a handle on it. Treat this as a claim under review, not an accepted result.
