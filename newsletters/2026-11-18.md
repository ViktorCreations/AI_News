# AI News — Wednesday, November 18, 2026

> Nvidia's first earnings since Jensen Huang's exit show revenue doubling to $115.84 billion, even as the board rules out his return and the company stops selling H200 chips in China; OpenAI answers with GPT-6.2, and Anthropic's IPO filing reveals who owns what.

## Top Stories

### [Nvidia reports $115.84B in quarterly revenue, says it won't reinstate Huang, and ends H200 sales in China](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/nov/17/nvidia-third-quarter-revenue-jensen-huang)
The Guardian reports revenue beat expectations at $115.84 billion, double a year earlier, and that board chair Harvey Jones said Nvidia "does not intend to reinstate Jensen as CEO." Interim CEO Colette Kress delivered the results. The stock, down more than 10% since the scandal to a $3.7 trillion valuation from nearly $6 trillion in October, rose about 5% after hours.

[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/17/nvidia-is-abandoning-china-after-jensen-huangs-ouster/) reports Kress said Nvidia will no longer sell H200 chips in China, and that the board created a committee to oversee national security policy and export-control compliance. She said the backlog for chips like Vera Rubin means Monday's new rules on overseas data centers would not hurt revenue, though Jefferies estimates they could cut $44 billion to $62 billion from next year's data-center sales. Huang [said he would be "honored" to return](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/nov/17/nvidia-jensen-huang-statement) as CEO, per the Guardian's headline; that article's text was not read here.

### [OpenAI launches GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol, with lower API prices](https://the-decoder.com/openai-launches-gpt-6-2-astra-and-sol-with-lower-api-prices-and-some-benchmark-gains/)
OpenAI's [announcement](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-2-astra-sol) describes better professional outputs, coding, computer use and efficiency at lower cost across ChatGPT, Codex and the API. The Decoder reports Astra targets demanding work in science, law and finance and Sol everyday tasks, both are available to Plus, Pro, Enterprise and Edu users, and Astra's API price is 20% below its predecessor's. It says Artificial Analysis ranks Astra second, behind Claude Fable 5.2, on its Intelligence Index but best on cost-performance; the ranking is Artificial Analysis's.

## Capital & Deals

- **[Amazon and Google will together own nearly 30% of Anthropic after its IPO](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/17/amazon-and-google-will-together-own-nearly-30-of-anthropic-after-its-ipo/)**: Anthropic's filing shows Amazon at 14.7% in non-voting shares, with enough unconverted notes to reach 23.8% if all were converted, and Google at 13.7%. A group including AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia and TSMC Partners agreed to buy $26 billion of non-voting stock, Google and Nvidia $10 billion each; Anthropic also committed to spend over $100 billion on AWS over the next decade.
- **[Alphabet's Isomorphic Labs raises $5B in its second mega-round in six months](https://the-decoder.com/isomorphic-labs-raises-5-billion-in-its-second-mega-round-within-six-months/)**: Greenoaks and Sequoia co-led, with Andreessen Horowitz new and Alphabet participating, after $2.1B in May. The money goes to its IsoDDE drug-design platform and moving its pipeline toward clinical trials; it has partnerships with Novartis, Eli Lilly and Johnson & Johnson but no approved drug.
