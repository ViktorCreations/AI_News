# AI News — Saturday, November 28, 2026

> OpenAI sues Apple in a trade-secrets countersuit, new documents place banned Nvidia Blackwell hardware in a Shanghai data center tied to the Megaspeed founder, and experts speculate, without evidence, that AI labs may be sitting on cryptography breakthroughs.

## Top Stories

### [OpenAI countersues Apple over trade secrets and alleged coercion of shared suppliers](https://the-decoder.com/openai-countersues-apple-over-trade-secret-theft-and-alleged-coercion-of-shared-suppliers/)
The Decoder, citing the Financial Times, reports OpenAI filed suit Friday in a California court, accusing Apple of trade secret theft and unfair business practices, of "coercing and threatening" suppliers that work with both companies, and of using its market power to stifle competition. The complaint also alleges Apple encourages employees to store corporate data on personal devices and then turns that practice against staff who want to leave, and that a former OpenAI employee passed product and supplier details to Apple after returning there. This follows Apple's own summer suit accusing former employees of leaking information to OpenAI; OpenAI closed its $6.4 billion purchase of Jony Ive's io studio and is preparing devices to rival Apple. These are OpenAI's allegations; Apple's response was not available in the source read.

### [Bloomberg: banned Nvidia Blackwell chips are installed in a Shanghai data center linked to Alice Huang](https://the-decoder.com/banned-nvidia-blackwell-chips-turn-up-in-a-shanghai-data-center-linked-to-alice-huang-bloomberg-reports/)
Per The Decoder's account of the Bloomberg report, filings with Chinese regulators show Shanghai Shuoyao offered a GB300-NVL72 rack as collateral for a 96 million yuan (about $14 million) loan and Shanghai Kezhan listed a GB200 server at the same site; Bloomberg traced the rack to Taiwanese maker Gigabyte through a serial number in a public warranty database. Shanghai Shuoyao is closely tied to Megaspeed, which Alice Huang founded in 2023 and Nvidia approved as a partner within four days, per Bloomberg, and which became Nvidia's largest customer in Southeast Asia. Exporting these chips to China without a license is banned; Megaspeed has denied wrongdoing, and this reporting adds to, but does not by itself establish, the smuggling allegations.

### [Experts warn AI labs may be sitting on cryptography "bombshells"; the evidence is rumor](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/nov/28/ai-artificial-intelligence-encryption-global-chaos)
The Guardian reports computer scientist Scott Aaronson says he is "virtually certain, from multiple reliable sources, that the AI labs are now sitting on one or more bombshells [in cryptography]," and that "well-attested rumours" say AI models have made progress on decades-old maths problems related to how secrets are encoded. Mathematician Marcus du Sautoy said "maybe they've already solved it" and that it may have been kept quiet. Nothing has been published or confirmed, and no lab has been quoted; the piece itself notes governments and banks have been preparing for decryption advances because of quantum computing. Treat it as speculation prompted by the recent run of AI maths results, including the [Riemann hypothesis claim](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/nov/21/anthropic-riemann-hypothesis-solved-claude-ai).
