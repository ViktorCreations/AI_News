# AI News — Monday, November 16, 2026

> The Nvidia fallout shifts from Jensen Huang to the chips: the Wall Street Journal reports a firm tied to his ex-lover brokered nearly $12 billion of orders for China, and a senator accuses the Commerce Department of letting restricted chips flow.

## Top Stories

### [Megaspeed reportedly brokered nearly $12B of Nvidia chip orders for Chinese buyers](https://the-decoder.com/firm-linked-to-jensen-huangs-ex-lover-reportedly-brokered-nearly-12-billion-in-nvidia-chip-orders-for-china/)
The Decoder, relaying the Wall Street Journal, reports that documents show Singapore-based Megaspeed arranged nearly $12 billion of orders for several hundred thousand export-controlled Nvidia chips for Chinese companies between March 2024 and June 2025, and says Alice Huang controls the company. Megaspeed told Reuters that she left on August 30, 2025, and has no current role, that it conducted "legitimate and lawful business," and that it has hired outside counsel for an internal investigation. [The BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cmdx1vpprk9vo) reports Megaspeed said reports about its business were "not supported by verifiable evidence."

That the chips reached China is the WSJ's reporting and is not independently confirmed here; the allegation that Alice Huang is a Chinese spy also remains unconfirmed. The Decoder adds that Nvidia reported $12.4 billion of China revenue over six months (7% of the total), while short-seller Culper Research estimates more than 20% of last fiscal year's revenue came from Chinese customers.

### [Sen. Cantwell accuses Commerce of letting AI chips flow to China](https://www.theverge.com/policy/1034047/nvidia-us-commerce-department-cantwell-lutnick)
In a Sunday letter to Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick, Washington Democrat Maria Cantwell said the department "undermined America's national security," had reportedly not heeded White House and intelligence warnings to restrict Megaspeed's chip access, and allowed Alibaba and ByteDance to reach Nvidia chips through overseas data centers. This follows Friday's pressure on Lutnick from Treasury Secretary Bessent and Republican senators; the letter is a Democratic accusation, and Commerce's response was not available in the sources read.

### [Apple launches a study app on how people use AI for health, with OpenEvidence](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/15/apple-launches-new-app-to-study-how-people-use-ai-for-health/)
US users over 21 with an iPhone 15 or later, iOS 26 and Apple Intelligence can join a study of up to 200,000 people over more than 12 months. The app answers health questions with answers grounded in sources such as NEJM, JAMA and Nature, and participants may share HealthKit data. Apple says data is anonymized before going to a third-party cloud, will not be used to train models, and OpenEvidence deletes queries and responses after 24 hours.
