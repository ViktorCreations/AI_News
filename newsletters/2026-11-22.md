## Top Stories

### [Lean groups say they have verified the Claude-assisted proof of the Riemann hypothesis](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/nov/21/anthropic-riemann-hypothesis-solved-claude-ai)
This follows Saturday's issue, in which Anthropic mathematician Levent Alpöge said he and Claude had produced a proof. The Guardian reports that on Saturday afternoon the Lean Focused Research Organization and the Mathlib Initiative announced they had independently verified it by checking the Lean code he published, saying: "We have full confidence that Alpöge's proof of the Riemann hypothesis is correct." The Guardian's headline says two groups verified the claim, and that experts say it checks out; Alpöge himself noted the "horrible" timing, a day after Anthropic's market debut.

[The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/hours-after-anthropic-claims-a-claude-proof-of-the-riemann-hypothesis-stanford-releases-an-all-human-competing-draft/) adds that Alpöge says his method also resolves the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis, and that Stanford researchers published a separate, entirely human-written approach by mathematician Alessandro Masullo hours later. The verification concerns the published Lean code; as of Friday's [TechCrunch report](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/20/ai-claimed-a-solution-to-the-167-year-old-riemann-hypothesis-and-a-human-did-too/) the argument itself was not yet understood by humans, and I have not seen a newer account of that.

### [South Korea's education minister resigns after an AI cheating scandal hits the university entrance exams](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/nov/22/south-korea-education-minister-resigns-after-ai-cheating-scandal-engulfs-university-exams)
The Guardian reports Choi Kyo-jin said he "failed to prepare adequately" for the suneung exams, which this year had phones banned and checks for cameras hidden in clothing. The scandal began last Sunday when the top scorer in a national tax accountant exam confessed to using a miniature camera and smartphone to get a near-perfect score, four days before the suneung, prompting emergency measures such as blocking public wifi near testing areas.
