## Top Stories

### [Catching up (August 5): Hassabis steps aside as Google DeepMind CEO](https://fortune.com/2026/08/05/demis-hassabis-steps-down-google-deepmind-ai-shakeup/)
We covered Jeff Dean's departure for Discovery Loop on Thursday without noting it was one half of a single reshuffle. Announced August 5, Hassabis leaves the CEO role he has held since 2010 to become chairman of Google DeepMind and chief scientist of Alphabet, focusing on AGI strategy and spending more time on Isomorphic Labs. Koray Kavukcuoglu, previously CTO and Alphabet's chief AI architect, takes day-to-day control as senior vice president — reporting to Sundar Pichai rather than inheriting the CEO title, which flattens DeepMind's independence a little. The context is unflattering: Gemini 3.5 Pro is months past its June target, and Google has been losing people to OpenAI and Anthropic.

### [Humans approving agent commands miss roughly one dangerous request in three](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/08/06/humans-in-the-loop-miss-a-third-of-dangerous-ai-coding-agent-requests/5284236)
Belgian developer Alex Wauters built a 60-second browser game that shows players simulated Claude Code permission prompts and scores both wrongly approving risks and wrongly denying safe commands. Across more than 40,000 runs and 409,000 decisions, roughly one in three malicious commands got waved through, scope violations worst at 35%. The single most-missed was `npm run analyze`, approved nearly 65% of the time — it will run whatever `package.json` defines. Two findings compound: Anthropic telemetry has users approving about 93% of prompts, and accuracy degrades the more prompts a person sees. Human-in-the-loop is the industry's standard mitigation, and this is a measurement of how much load it can actually bear.

## Product & Industry

- **[Agent Plugins 1.0 lands — without the company that invented its two halves](https://developers.googleblog.com/agent-plugins-package-your-skills-tools-and-more/)** — published August 6 by a steering committee from Amazon, Cursor, Microsoft, OpenAI and Vercel, with Google since joining as a core maintainer, the vendor-neutral spec packages Agent Skills and MCP servers into portable plugins: a directory with a `plugin.json` manifest, a `skills/` folder and an `mcp.json`. Agent Skills originated at Anthropic and MCP was donated by it to the Linux Foundation — and Anthropic is not among the listed maintainers of the standard built on both.

## Compute & Data Centers

- **[A planned Amazon data centre could become the single largest source of US climate pollution](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/08/planned-amazon-data-center-could-become-the-biggest-climate-polluter-in-the-u-s/)** — per New York Times reporting, the on-site natural gas plant for Amazon's Pecos County, Texas facility is permitted to emit 33 million tons of CO2 a year, which would exceed any existing US power plant. Amazon's emissions rose 16% last year, away from its 2040 net-zero pledge, which the company attributes partly to AI demand.

## Capital & Deals

- **[AMD is buying Taalas, which etches model weights into silicon](https://www.theregister.com/systems/2026/08/06/amd-acquires-ai-chip-startup-taalas-to-boost-inference-performance-by-etching-models-into-silicon/5284344)** — terms undisclosed, closing expected in Q4 subject to approval. The Toronto startup, founded 2023, builds model-specific integrated circuits that bake weights into the die instead of streaming them from HBM; its HC1 test chip served Llama 3.1 8B at 16,960 tokens/second on TSMC 6nm. AMD plans to pair them with Helios racks, GPUs handling prompts and Taalas parts handling generation. The catch is inherent: a chip is locked to its model, and a new one means re-spinning silicon — reportedly two metal layers rather than a full redesign.
- **[OpenAI acquires presentation startup NextSlide](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/08/openai-acquires-presentation-startup-nextslide/)** — terms undisclosed and the deal actually closed earlier this year, disclosed only now; founder Ahmed Beshry's team, which turned prompts and documents into editable decks, is now working on ChatGPT.
