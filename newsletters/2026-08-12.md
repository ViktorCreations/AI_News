# AI News — Wednesday, August 12, 2026

> Ten days after the EU's labelling rules took effect, Anthropic will start watermarking Claude's text output — not just its files. It also put 60 subagents and 31 million tokens against the Riemann hypothesis, and moved the number.

## Top Stories

### [An unreleased Anthropic model pushed the Riemann hypothesis's verified bound higher](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/11/an-unreleased-anthropic-model-made-progress-on-one-of-maths-biggest-unsolved-problems/)
Not a proof, and worth stating plainly: the model significantly raised the lower bound below which the 150-year-old hypothesis is known to hold, rather than settling it. The method is the interesting part — 650 different approaches tried across 60 subagents, 31 million output tokens, about 36 hours of wall clock. Two Anthropic staff mathematicians checked the result and it was formalised in Lean. It is the second frontier lab in two weeks to publish machine-verified mathematics, after OpenAI's Astra shipped ten results on August 1, and the same pattern holds: the claim comes with a certificate, so the field can check it without trusting the lab.

### [Anthropic will watermark the text its models generate](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/11/anthropic-says-it-will-watermark-text-generated-by-its-ai-models/)
Every Claude model released after August 2 carries a model-level watermark in the text itself, across the API, Claude, Claude Code, Claude Cowork and Claude Tag, with C2PA for files and older models to follow. The date is not a coincidence: August 2 is when the EU AI Act's Article 50 transparency duties became enforceable, requiring machine-readable marking of generated content. The honest caveat is in the detail — the mark survives copy-paste and "may persist through some editing," with no stated threshold at which it stops surviving.

### [Gemini reaches a billion monthly users](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/11/googles-gemini-app-surges-to-one-billion-users/)
Sundar Pichai posted the number on Tuesday: the 14th Google product to reach a billion, up from 650 million last October and over 950 million at July's earnings call. Alongside it, 63% of users touch voice, the app generates over 150 million images a day, and iOS alone accounts for over 100 million actives. ChatGPT reached the same monthly milestone back in June, and reported a billion *weekly* users last week — so Gemini has caught up on the coarser measure while OpenAI is now quoting a harder one. Either way the contest has moved to distribution rather than novelty.

## Product & Industry

- **[Spotify will badge "AI Persona" artists and drop them from recommendations](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/11/spotify-will-label-ai-persona-profiles-and-exclude-their-music-from-recommendations/)** — announced Tuesday with self-disclosure open now and badges appearing in mid-September, the policy covers profiles presenting a photorealistic AI-generated identity rather than a person. Those profiles are excluded by default from editorial, algorithmic and personalised recommendations, surfacing only for users who follow or search them. Spotify is explicit that this is about who the artist claims to be, not how the music was made.

## Compute & Data Centers

- **[Anthropic, Macquarie and GIC form Theseus Infrastructure](https://www.macquarie.com/us/en/about/news/2026/anthropic-mam-gic-data-centre-infrastructure-partnership.html)** — announced August 10, the platform will develop, operate and lease data centres to Anthropic as anchor tenant on long-term agreements, with Macquarie Asset Management funds and GIC owning it and funding most of the equity per project, focused initially on the US.
