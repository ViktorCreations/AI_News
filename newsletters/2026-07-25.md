# AI News — Saturday, July 25, 2026

> Two kinds of scale defined the day: Anthropic shipped Claude Opus 5, claiming near-frontier performance at half the price of the model it beats, while South Korea became the buildout's newest center of gravity with more than $500 billion of Nvidia-linked commitments signed in a single week.

## Top Stories

### [Anthropic ships Claude Opus 5](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-5)
Released July 24 at $5 per million input tokens and $25 per million output, unchanged from Opus 4.8. Anthropic says the model surpasses all others on Frontier-Bench v0.1 while more than doubling Opus 4.8's score, lands within 0.5% of Fable 5's peak on CursorBench 3.2 at half the cost, and scores three times the next-best model on ARC-AGI 3. It also calls Opus 5 its most aligned model to date, at 2.3 on its automated behavioral audit for misaligned behavior — the lowest of any recent Claude.

### [Nvidia and SK Group sign a $500-billion-plus AI infrastructure partnership](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/sk-group-nvidia-ai-factories-memory)
Announced July 24: SK Telecom will build a 2-gigawatt-scale AI cloud in Korea on Nvidia's DSX platform, pairing Vera Rubin accelerators with SK hynix HBM4, with first operations targeted for 2027. SK hynix separately enters a long-term partnership to co-develop next-generation AI memory. Both sides signed letters of intent rather than binding contracts, so the headline figure is an intention, not a booked commitment.

### [OpenAI launches Health in ChatGPT for US users](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/07/24/chatgpt-wants-access-to-your-health-records/5278430)
Users 18 and over on web and iOS can now connect Apple Health data and supported medical records, letting ChatGPT reference medications, lab results, and recent visits in conversation; OpenAI says the data trains no models and targets no ads. It is a second attempt — a January pilot saw users conduct more than 70% of health conversations outside the dedicated space — and it arrives alongside research finding chatbots no better than search engines for health decisions.

## Research & Models

- **[Google publishes ATLAS v1.0, mapping Gemini use across the economy](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/research/understanding-the-ai-economy/)** — 15 million de-identified interactions across 800 occupations and 4,000 tasks find AI reaching 68% of occupations but a median of just 21% of tasks within them, with fewer than 10% of workplace conversations aimed at full automation.
- **[FLUX 3 (Black Forest Labs)](https://bfl.ai/blog/flux-3)** — the July 23 early-access release is trained jointly on image, video, and audio, generating 20-second clips with native synchronized sound, and extends to robotic action prediction via a FLUX-mimic variant.
- **[ABot-World-0](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19191)** — trending on Hugging Face this week: Alibaba AMAP's world model turns a single RTX 5090 into a real-time interactive simulator, running open-ended action-conditioned rollout at 720p and 16 FPS within about 19 GiB of VRAM.

## Product & Industry

- **[Zenity Labs details "AgentForger," a ChatGPT agent-forgery flaw](https://thehackernews.com/2026/07/chatgpt-agentforger-flaw-could-deploy.html)** — the research, published July 23–24, shows how a single crafted link could make ChatGPT's Agent Builder stand up an invisible, attacker-controlled agent holding a real employee's access with approvals disabled; Zenity reported it on June 4 and OpenAI patched it by June 8, with no sign of exploitation in the wild.
- **[Nvidia and KAIST open a joint AI research lab in Seoul](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-kaist-joint-ai-research-lab)** — announced July 23 at KAIST's Kim Jaechul Graduate School of AI, the lab is dedicated to advancing agentic AI for South Korea.

## Compute & Data Centers

- **[NAVER, Nvidia and Brookfield commit $10B to expand Korea's sovereign AI factory](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/25/3333160/0/en/NAVER-NVIDIA-and-Brookfield-to-Expand-Korea-s-National-AI-Factory-Infrastructure-Buildout.html)** — the GAK Sejong deployment grows from 55 MW to 200 MW by 2028, funded by up to $9B from Brookfield and $1B from Nvidia, with NAVER targeting 1 GW long-term.

## Regulation & Policy

- **[Reps. Lieu and Moran introduce the AI Kill Switch Act](https://lieu.house.gov/media-center/press-releases/reps-lieu-and-moran-introduce-bill-require-kill-switch-ai-systems-can)** — the July 23 bill would require developers of the most powerful models to retain the technical ability to throttle, suspend, or shut them down, and lets the Homeland Security secretary — consulting Commerce and the DNI — order a graduated slowdown or full shutdown of a system capable of catastrophic harm, alongside incident reporting and forensic record preservation.
