# AI News — Friday, August 7, 2026

> OpenAI's answer to Apple's trade-secrets suit is not that nothing was taken — it is that Apple guarded the material so loosely it was never a trade secret. Meanwhile ChatGPT's free tier stops counting messages.

## Top Stories

### [OpenAI's defence: Apple's own security practices sink its trade-secrets claim](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/06/openai-says-apples-own-security-practices-undermine-its-trade-secrets-case/)
A day after Apple told the court that about 11 more ex-employees may have carried data over, OpenAI moved to dismiss — and its argument sidesteps the question of what left. Trade-secret protection requires reasonable efforts to keep a secret, and OpenAI's exhibits allege Apple let staff use personal iCloud accounts for work, failed to revoke access on departure, and — per submitted text messages — left a manager logged into the personal iCloud account of departed engineer Chang Liu to transfer files, later asking him for help with Apple technical questions. OpenAI calls these "inexplicable information-management practices."

### [ChatGPT's free tier goes unlimited, on GPT-5.6 Luna](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/06/openai-brings-unlimited-chatgpt-text-chats-to-free-users/)
Luna replaces GPT-5.5 as the default for Free and Go users this week; unlimited text chats and a "Think" button for harder questions follow next week. Separate limits stay on files, images, voice and image generation. Read alongside the 80% cut to Luna's API rate on July 30, it reads as OpenAI converting cheaper inference into reach rather than margin — and it lands just after ChatGPT crossed a billion weekly users.

## Research & Models

- **[Liquid AI's LFM2.5-2.6B puts an open-weight agent model on devices](https://www.liquid.ai/blog/lfm2-5-2-6b)** — announced August 4 and picked up widely this week: 2.6B parameters trained on ~34T tokens, released for download and fine-tuning without restriction, running at 220 tok/s on an M5 Max, 113 on a Ryzen AI Max+ 395 and about 30 on a phone. Liquid claims it is competitive with, and often beats, models nearly four times its size on instruction-following and tool use, while conceding that coding-heavy or complex agentic work still wants something larger. It is the model behind the MacPaw on-device deal we covered yesterday.

## Product & Industry

- **[Google Maps turns Ask Maps into an agent](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/06/google-maps-adds-agentic-features-including-food-ordering-and-hotel-bookings/)** — ordering food through Square, Toast or Uber Eats, booking hotels and finding event tickets, all rolling out in the US, plus conversation memory and a Personal Intelligence mode that draws on Gmail and Calendar. That last one is off by default, which is the right call and worth noting because it so often is not.
- **[Suno will watermark its AI songs](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/06/amid-legal-battles-suno-says-it-will-start-watermarking-songs/)** — audio watermarking and fingerprinting to stop tracks circulating undetected on other platforms, download limits, revised community guidelines, and a deal with Musixmatch to use its Sentinel copyright-detection system. Suno would not say which watermarking standard it will use. The context is a German court ruling against it in July, RIAA-coordinated suits from Universal and Sony, and a class action over a 2025 breach affecting 55 million users.

## Compute & Data Centers

- **[Google's $15B Visakhapatnam data centre runs into water and wildlife litigation](https://thenextweb.com/news/google-india-data-centre-water-wildlife-visakhapatnam)** — built with Gautam Adani's group and pitched by the state as up to 188,000 jobs, the project faces environmental court challenges and a High Court hearing later this month. The city already supplies 410 million litres a day against 480 million of demand, and the Kambalakonda Wildlife Sanctuary — leopards, pangolins — sits 860 metres away. Protest banners read "We cannot drink DATA," which is the compute buildout's social licence problem stated in four words.

## Capital & Deals

- **[Mirendil signs a $100M+ Google Cloud deal for self-improving AI](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/06/exclusive-mirendil-inks-100m-google-cloud-deal-to-scale-self-improving-ai/)** — the lab founded by Anthropic alumni Behnam Neyshabur and Harsh Mehta commits over half the $200M seed it raised in June, at a $1B valuation, to a multiyear deal for TPUs, Nvidia GPUs and managed training clusters. The pitch is systems that keep researching and improving their own performance in a domain — Neyshabur cites Alzheimer's research — which is the second recursive-self-improvement startup to surface this week, after Jeff Dean's Discovery Loop.
