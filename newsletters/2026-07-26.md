# AI News — Sunday, July 26, 2026

> The industry split in public over how America should answer China: twenty-five companies led by Nvidia, Microsoft and Meta signed a letter against restricting open-weight models — and the three closed-model frontier labs stayed off it.

## Top Stories

### [Nvidia, Microsoft and Meta lead a 25-company letter against open-weight restrictions](https://thenextweb.com/news/open-weights-american-ai-leadership-letter-huang-nvidia-openai-absent)
The July 24 letter, "Open Weights and American AI Leadership," argues US leadership depends on a strong open ecosystem rather than a single frontier model, and asks Washington for targeted rules against unlawful model extraction instead of sweeping bans. Signatories include Mistral, Palantir, IBM, Andreessen Horowitz, Hugging Face, Mozilla and the Linux Foundation; OpenAI, Anthropic and Google did not sign. Jensen Huang made the letter the subject of his first-ever post on X, which drew more than 11 million views. It follows the White House's accusation that Moonshot distilled Anthropic's model to build Kimi K3, and Treasury's threat of sanctions over the practice.

### [Monday.com cuts 20% of its workforce in an AI restructuring](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/22/monday-com-lays-off-hundreds-to-focuses-on-ai/)
The July 22 cut of roughly 630 employees, framed as a shift to a "leaner, more focused operating model" around its AI Work Platform, carries $45–55M in restructuring charges. It lands inside a broader pattern: 78% of companies laying off workers in 2026 have cited an AI refocus as a reason.

## Compute & Data Centers

- **[One fallen power line exposed how AI data centers destabilize the grid](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/25/one-fallen-power-line-exposed-a-growing-ai-data-center-problem-heres-how-to-fix-it/)** — when a line went down near Washington, DC, more than 3 GW of data centers switched to backup power within seconds, causing voltage spikes across PJM from Northern Virginia to Chicago; the event was twice the size of a similar 2024 incident, and data centers are projected to grow from 6% of PJM's load to 24% by 2040.
- **[AMD and Cerebras split inference across two chip architectures](https://newsroom.amd.com/news/aai-2026-cerebras-inference/)** — announced July 23, the disaggregated design puts prompt processing on AMD's Helios racks and token generation on Cerebras's Wafer-Scale Engine, which the companies model at up to 5x higher tokens per second per watt than Cerebras alone; it arrives first on Cerebras Cloud in H2 2026.
- **[Intel says it must "leapfrog" ARM and AMD, and bets on edge AI and robotics](https://www.theregister.com/systems/2026/07/24/intel-knows-it-needs-to-leapfrog-arm-and-amd-says-ceo-lip-bu-tan/5277968)** — on a Q2 call reporting $16.1B in revenue, up 25% year on year, CEO Lip-Bu Tan conceded Intel is still behind in some areas and folded the PC business into a new Client Computing and Physical AI Group, assuming edge AI and robotics will eventually rival the PC market in size.
