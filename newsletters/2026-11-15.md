# AI News — Sunday, November 15, 2026

> A quiet weekend with the Nvidia story still running: a petition to reinstate Jensen Huang gathers more than 115,000 signatures, a Chinese lab releases a large open model it says never touched an Nvidia GPU, and Anthropic's CEO describes compute purchasing as his "deepest business dilemma" days before its IPO.

## Top Stories

### [A petition to reinstate Jensen Huang passes 115,000 signatures, with Elon Musk among the signers](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/14/elon-musk-other-tech-execs-sign-petition-calling-on-nvidia-to-reinstate-jensen-huang/)
TechCrunch reports the petition appears to have attracted more than 115,000 signatures as of Saturday afternoon. Its site says signers include 5,965 current Nvidia employees, 1,108 former employees and 63,063 shareholders, and Musk, Replit's Amjad Masad, Epic's Tim Sweeney and Y Combinator's Garry Tan are named supporters. It argues Huang "was removed without meaningful due process" and asks the board to bring him back while an independent investigation establishes the facts.

These counts come from the petition's own site and TechCrunch says they "appear" to be accurate; they are not independently verified. Huang resigned on Thursday, [as reported Friday](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/nov/13/nvidia-scandal-sparks-us-national-security-concerns-china), and has agreed to testify to Congress. TechCrunch's [roundup of Asian reactions](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/14/asian-governments-react-to-nvidia-ceo-jensen-huangs-exit/) says China's foreign ministry called the spy allegations groundless, and cites Reuters that his exit raises uncertainty over more than $3 billion of Chinese AI computing projects reliant on restricted Nvidia chips.

### [Meituan releases weights for LongCat-2.5, a 1.6-trillion-parameter agent model it says was trained entirely on Chinese hardware](https://the-decoder.com/meituan-releases-a-1-6-trillion-parameter-agent-model-that-never-touched-an-nvidia-gpu/)
The food-delivery company's mixture-of-experts model activates about 48 billion parameters per token, handles up to one million tokens of context and accepts text, images and video. The Decoder says it performs at roughly the level Claude Opus 5 reached a few months ago on coding benchmarks; the benchmark claims are Meituan's. It arrives a day after a researcher [alleged](https://the-decoder.com/former-openai-researcher-says-he-found-nvidia-blackwell-fingerprints-hidden-inside-six-chinese-ai-models/) that other Chinese models were trained on export-controlled Blackwell chips, and the no-Nvidia claim is likewise the company's own.

### [Australia's central bank finds AI is hitting young workers first](https://the-decoder.com/australias-central-bank-finds-ai-hitting-young-workers-first-especially-in-software-and-customer-service/)
A Reserve Bank of Australia analysis finds AI has barely displaced any jobs overall, but job postings have fallen across all groups since ChatGPT launched in late 2022, most steeply in the most AI-exposed occupations such as programming, clerical work and customer service. In that group, workers aged 15 to 24 and young graduates have weaker employment rates and higher unemployment than older workers, per The Decoder's summary.

## Research & Models

- **[Gemini 4 Carbon takes first place on MathArena](https://the-decoder.com/gemini-4-carbon-takes-the-matharena-crown-but-costs-15-times-more-than-gpt-6-1-sol/)**: it scored 85.4% (±3.0), with Claude Fable 5.2 second and GPT-6.1 Sol, the previous leader, third. At the reasoning settings tested, Carbon and Fable cost roughly 15 and 40 times as much as GPT-6.1 Sol, and Carbon's most common failure is exceeding the time limit. OpenAI's most advanced math models are not publicly available.

## Capital & Deals

- **[Amodei: compute purchasing is Anthropic's "deepest business dilemma," with commitments near $901B](https://the-decoder.com/anthropic-ceo-amodei-admits-theres-a-huge-uncertainty-in-whats-going-to-happen-next-year-days-before-a-record-ipo/)**: ahead of the IPO, he said capacity must be bought one to two years before anyone knows whether revenue will justify it. Bloomberg, per The Decoder, puts commitments at nearly $901B, mostly compute and chips, with about $71B due by September 2027; the IPO is meant to build a cash buffer against a mismatch.
