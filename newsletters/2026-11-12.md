## Top Stories

### [Nvidia's board is reportedly preparing to remove Jensen Huang, and Congress moves toward a subpoena](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/11/nvidias-stock-drops-as-board-reportedly-preps-to-oust-ceo-jensen-huang/)
The Wall Street Journal reported, per TechCrunch, that Nvidia's board was meeting as soon as Wednesday evening to decide Huang's future, and the stock fell about 8% in after-hours trading. Bloomberg reports Nvidia is working with an outside firm to identify possible successors; TechCrunch notes the company has no obvious succession plan. As of Thursday morning, none of the sources read here reported a decision, and the BBC said Nvidia and its board members did not reply to requests for comment.

This follows Sunday's [board inquiry](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/08/nvidia-says-its-investigating-ceo-jensen-huangs-relationship-with-possible-chinese-spy) and Huang's admission of an affair with an alleged Chinese spy. New on Wednesday: the New York Times [reported](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/11/jensen-huang-accused-of-having-another-affair-with-a-nvidia-exec/) that he is also suspected of an affair with a direct report, citing five employees; that is an allegation, not a confirmed fact. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/11/congress-to-call-jensen-huang-in-to-answer-for-alleged-chinese-spy-affair/) adds that House China committee chairman John Moolenaar said Huang had until the end of Wednesday to agree to a closed-door briefing or likely face a subpoena, and House Foreign Affairs chairman Brian Mast said his committee is preparing one.

### [Anthropic says AI cut its AMD chip port from months to three days](https://the-decoder.com/anthropic-says-ai-is-eroding-nvidias-cuda-lead-cutting-an-amd-chip-switch-from-months-to-three-days/)
According to The Decoder, co-founder Tom Brown said Anthropic now trains and runs Claude on all four major chip families, treating them as one interchangeable pool. When porting to AMD GPUs, its frontier model Mythos reportedly wrote or ported nearly 100% of the kernels in about three days, against the usual six to twelve months. These are company claims, relayed second-hand; the point for the industry is that a software moat like Nvidia's CUDA gets cheaper to cross as models write the low-level code.

### [DeepMind's AlphaProof Code verified Signal's cryptographic core in four weeks](https://the-decoder.com/google-deepminds-alphaproof-code-verified-signals-cryptographic-core-in-four-weeks-instead-of-years/)
Per The Decoder, summarising a DeepMind blog post, the system mathematically proved that the actual code running on users' devices meets its security specifications, work that typically takes years; earlier efforts stopped at the protocol's theoretical design. Signal's CTO says it strengthens trust in privacy as AI threats grow, and DeepMind plans to extend the method to power grids and medical devices.

## Product & Industry

- **[Google launches the Gemini Decisions API](https://the-decoder.com/google-joins-the-decision-model-wave-with-gemini-decision-1-scoring-1000-questions-in-a-single-pass/)**: the Gemini Decision 1 model answers up to 1,000 closed-ended questions (yes/no, multiple choice, ratings) in a single pass across text, images, audio, video and PDFs, returning probabilities in 150 to 250 milliseconds. It costs $0.10 per million input tokens with no output charge, which Google says is about five times cheaper than Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite, and is available in AI Studio.

## Capital & Deals

- **[OpenAI's quarterly revenue reportedly passed $10B for the first time](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/11/openais-quarterly-revenue-reportedly-crosses-10b-for-the-first-time/)**: Bloomberg, citing anonymous sources, puts third-quarter revenue about 50% above the second quarter's $6.7B, with adjusted operating margin of -109% against -150%. - **[UK chip startup Fractile raises $720M](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/11/with-720m-round-britains-fractile-gets-a-leg-up-in-the-race-for-ai-chips/)**: the round was led by Thrive Capital, Founders Fund, Lightspeed and Redpoint at a valuation the UK government calls "over £5 billion," which the FT and Reuters source put at about $7B.
