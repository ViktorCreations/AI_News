# AI News — Tuesday, July 28, 2026

> The largest open-weight model ever built actually shipped — and the same day, Anthropic published a rebuttal to the charge that it wants models like it banned.

## Top Stories

### [Moonshot ships Kimi K3's weights — the largest open-weight release yet](https://huggingface.co/moonshotai/Kimi-K3)
The weights went live at 00:00 UTC on July 27, eleven days after the model itself launched, and the repository has already logged roughly 99,000 downloads. The 2.8-trillion-parameter mixture-of-experts model activates 16 of 896 experts per token, ships in quantization-aware MXFP4 at about 1.4TB, and comes with a full technical report. "Open" carries conditions, though: the Kimi K3 License requires a separate agreement for model-as-a-service businesses above $20M in revenue over any 12 months, and products above 100 million monthly users or $20M in monthly revenue must display "Kimi K3" in their interface.

### [Anthropic: we have never advocated banning open-weight models](https://www.anthropic.com/news/position-open-weights-models)
Dario Amodei published Anthropic's position on Monday, days after the company sat out the 25-signatory open-weights letter we covered July 26 and was read as favouring restrictions. The post says Anthropic "has never advocated for a ban on open-weights models" as a category, and names what it does want instead: keeping advanced chips out of authoritarian hands, stopping industrial-scale distillation, and mandatory safety testing for all sufficiently capable models — open and closed alike. The stated worry is not openness but authoritarian governments fielding more capable systems than the US.
