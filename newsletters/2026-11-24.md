# AI News — Tuesday, November 24, 2026

> Nvidia reverses itself and reinstates Jensen Huang less than two weeks after he resigned, sending its shares up 31% and the Nasdaq up 6.5%, while Commerce moves to blacklist Megaspeed and Anthropic ships agents that can approve code.

## Top Stories

### [Nvidia reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO, president and director](https://siliconangle.com/2026/11/23/nvidia-reverses-course-reinstating-jensen-huang-as-ceo-president-and-director/)
SiliconANGLE reports the board voted unanimously to restore him effective immediately, saying it was "satisfied with his candor and ability to lead," had received input from "senior levels of United States government," and is "confident" he poses no national security risk. The announcement did not address the affairs that led to his resignation. This reverses the position taken [last Tuesday](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/nov/17/nvidia-third-quarter-revenue-jensen-huang), when chairman Harvey Jones said Nvidia "does not intend to reinstate Jensen as CEO." Huang said he was "honored to return."

[The BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cq62pn6j42jko) reports Nvidia's market value rose by nearly $1 trillion as shares jumped more than 31%, and that the Nasdaq rose 6.5% and the S&P 500 4.75%, with Micron up 19% and AMD nearly 7%; much of the rally started in pre-market trading before the announcement. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/23/jensen-huang-scheduled-to-testify-next-week-in-senate-following-reinstatement-as-nvidia-ceo/) reports Huang is scheduled to testify before the Senate Banking Committee on December 3.

### [The US says it will blacklist Megaspeed](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cv98xpg5dg5zo)
The BBC reports the Commerce Department announced it intends to add the Singapore-based data-center developer to its list of entities barred from buying or selling goods in the US, without saying when it takes effect. Megaspeed, whose former executive Alice Huang is alleged to have worked for Chinese intelligence, has denied wrongdoing and says its business is "legitimate and lawful." Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent had said earlier that he had favored the listing for months, as [reported on November 13](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/nov/13/nvidia-scandal-sparks-us-national-security-concerns-china); the BBC had no comment from Commerce, the White House or Megaspeed.

### [Anthropic launches Claude Ship, with agents that can approve code changes automatically](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/23/anthropic-launches-claude-ship-letting-ai-agents-review-fix-and-approve-code-changes/)
The beta adds two agents to the already available Code Review: PR Steward, which fixes failing CI checks, merge conflicts and review feedback and comments on what it changed and how to roll it back, and Claude Approvals, which can approve changes based on policies a team sets. TechCrunch calls the delegation of approval authority to an AI agent the notable part, and quotes Anthropic saying that on the Claude Code team "Claude writes most of our code."
