# AI News — Friday, November 13, 2026

> Jensen Huang has resigned as Nvidia's CEO, the board having reportedly been about to remove him, and a global Microsoft outage is knocking out email, Copilot and some OpenAI and Anthropic tools.

## Top Stories

### [Jensen Huang resigns as Nvidia CEO; CFO Colette Kress takes over on an interim basis](https://the-decoder.com/jensen-huang-resigns-as-nvidia-ceo-after-espionage-scandal-wipes-out-1-4-trillion-in-market-value/)
The Decoder reports that Huang resigned as CEO, president and board member effective immediately on Thursday, after the Wall Street Journal reported the board was planning to fire him over two extramarital affairs, one with a suspected Chinese spy. Kress is interim CEO while the board searches for a permanent successor, and Harvey C. Jones becomes the first independent board chair. The Decoder says Nvidia has lost roughly $1.4 trillion in market value since the first spy report on October 29. [The BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/ckgel338xql3o) also reports the resignation.

Huang [has agreed to testify to Congress](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/12/jensen-huang-agrees-to-testify-before-congress/), House Foreign Affairs chairman Brian Mast said, after lawmakers threatened a subpoena. TechCrunch also relays a Reuters report of a [third alleged affair](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/12/jensen-huang-was-having-a-third-affair-reuters-reports/), with an employee; that is an allegation not independently confirmed here.

### [A global Microsoft outage is disrupting email, Copilot and some AI services](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cwvg238zrnm5o)
The BBC reports Microsoft 365 services including Outlook, Teams and Copilot have had "connectivity problems" since Thursday evening, with Downdetector showing 140,000 reports of Outlook issues from 74 countries, a figure that undercounts the real total. Microsoft says it was not a cyber-attack and blames "elevated load" after many servers became unexpectedly unavailable; on Friday morning it reported "early signs of stabilisation" but capacity "not yet returned to normal." OpenAI and Anthropic both reported problems caused by it, and UK GP surgeries, pharmacies and police forces were affected.

### [Google releases Gemini 4 Hydrogen, a lower-cost model that it says outscores its flagship Argon](https://siliconangle.com/2026/11/12/google-releases-gemini-4-hydrogen-a-lower-cost-model-said-to-outscore-argon/)
Google says Hydrogen scores 51 against Argon's 50 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at default settings, and 54 at a new "xhigh" thinking level. Unlike Argon, which went first to vetted cybersecurity defenders, Hydrogen is available to everyone from today, including free Gemini app users. Google says it finished tasks 2.5 times faster than Gemini 3.8 Flash and scored 57% on Terminal-Bench 4.1, against 18.2% for that model. These are Google's own figures.

## AI in Real Estate

- **[The American Real Estate Association launches an AI Task Force to push "licensee-in-the-loop" laws in all 50 states](https://www.housingwire.com/articles/ara-licensee-in-loop-ai-laws/)**: announced November 12, per HousingWire. The proposal would require a named, licensed professional to supervise any automated system acting for a consumer in a real-estate transaction and to review offers before they go out; the task force has yet to hold its first meeting.
