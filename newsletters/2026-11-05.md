# AI News — Thursday, November 5, 2026

> The midterms are over and AI is already part of the aftermath: Democrats now control Congress, data-center backlash helped decide races, and AI-industry money bought mixed results. Meanwhile the "agentic web" still barely registers in Uber's and DoorDash's order books.

## Top Stories

### [Democrats take Congress, and data-center backlash looks set to shape what comes next](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/04/after-the-midterms-a-bigger-clash-over-data-centers-could-be-coming/)
TechCrunch reports that the 3 November results gave Democrats full control of Congress, with Republicans still in the White House, and that anger over data centers became a ballot-box issue. It cites an NBC exit poll in which more than half of respondents thought AI will do more harm than good, almost 80% opposed data centers in their neighborhood, and almost 60% called data centers a crucial or major factor in their vote.

Candidates who opposed rapid buildout won seats in Texas, Maine, Michigan, Nevada, Georgia and South Carolina, and an Ohio county where Meta had planned a facility passed a ban. TechCrunch expects more construction moratoriums and rules making data centers pay for their own grid upgrades, and notes that a Sanders and Ocasio-Cortez bill for a nationwide moratorium would face a possible veto. Goldman Sachs predicts construction delays in Georgia and Texas.

### [AI-industry and AI-safety PACs had mixed results in the midterms](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/04/ai-pacs-spent-millions-trying-to-shape-congress-heres-how-they-fared/)
Two industry PACs, American Mission and Building the Future, spent $4.8M backing South Carolina Senate candidate Darline Graham, who lost; American Mission, the Republican arm of the OpenAI president- and Andreessen Horowitz-backed group Leading the Future, also lost races in Montana and North Carolina. Its Democratic arm did better, backing Florida's Jared Moskowitz to re-election.

On the other side, Public First, backed by Anthropic, saw several Republican picks lose but did better with Democrats, and Guardrails Alliance, which treats AI money as a liability, spent over $1M against each of two candidates who lost. TechCrunch notes it is hard to separate AI spending from the broader Democratic wave, and that Leading the Future spent $27.38M in primaries, where it sees better value.

### [MIT researchers show GPT-6 Astra can read and rewrite other models' weights directly](https://the-decoder.com/gpt-6-astra-sees-the-matrix-and-reprograms-other-ai-models-directly-through-their-weights/)
In a research blog summarised by The Decoder, MIT researchers say GPT-6 Astra can analyze the weights of another model, working from raw lists of numbers or with programming tools and feedback from test runs, and modify them deliberately without a conventional training run. The Decoder says the approach has limits but could change how models are tuned and controlled.
