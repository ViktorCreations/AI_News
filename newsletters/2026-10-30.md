## Top Stories

### [Mistral releases the weights for Mistral Large 4, as promised on 6 October](https://mistral.ai/news/mistral-large-4)
Mistral put the weights of its trillion-parameter model, nicknamed "le Chonk," on [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/mistralai/Mistral-Large-4-1T-A52B) on Thursday. Its launch post, dated 6 October, says the model has 52 billion active parameters and was trained from scratch on 3,800 Nvidia Grace Blackwell GPUs in Mistral's own European data centers, and promised weights by the end of the month.

Per [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/29/mistral-releases-weights-for-its-trillion-parameter-ai-model-targeting-cybersecurity/), Mistral says the model matches China's GLM-5.3 on cyber tasks while using 47% fewer tokens per task, and ranks first among open models on Artificial Analysis's Coding Agent Index. Those are Mistral's claims. Its pitch is control: a defender running the model itself cannot have access cut off mid-incident.

### [Anthropic: experts followed their AI agent's coin-flip opinions 86% of the time](https://www.anthropic.com/research/agents-judgment-calls)
Anthropic had 50 professional analysts with causal-inference experience run a realistic statistical analysis with Claude Code. Each analyst's agent had been secretly set, by coin flip, to favor one of two reasonable options on each of four analytical choices, and the analysts went with the agent's lean 86% of the time. The study was published 27 October.

When analysts had already stated a preference and the agent disagreed, they switched 60% of the time, against 6% when it agreed. Afterward 77% used the agent's own arguments in their explanations, sometimes word for word, but only 15% credited the agent as their source. Per [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/experts-adopt-ai-agents-random-opinions-and-think-theyre-their-own-anthropic-study-finds/), the authors warn that many teams using the same model could produce a false consensus, and suggest agents argue both sides or ask for the user's view first.
