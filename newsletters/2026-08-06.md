# AI News — Thursday, August 6, 2026

> Google lost Jeff Dean and three of its most senior researchers in one go, to a startup that intends to point AI at running scientific experiments — and, unusually, Alphabet is backing it. Anthropic, meanwhile, started hiring people to design its own chips.

## Top Stories

### [Jeff Dean and three senior Google researchers leave to found Discovery Loop](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/05/jeff-dean-and-other-top-ai-researchers-are-leaving-google-to-launch-their-own-startup/)
Dean departs as CEO of the new venture, taking Sanjay Ghemawat, Quoc Le and Oriol Vinyals with him — a roster that between them shaped MapReduce, Bigtable, seq2seq and AlphaStar. Discovery Loop is structured as a public benefit corporation and aims to automate the experimental loop itself, running thousands of experiments in parallel and exploring recursive self-improvement. Radical Ventures and Khosla co-lead the first round, with Kleiner Perkins, Lightspeed and Doerr Capital participating — and Alphabet itself backing the company its researchers are leaving.

### [Anthropic starts building an in-house chip design team](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/05/anthropic-is-hiring-an-ai-chip-design-team/)
The company is recruiting silicon engineers to co-design hardware alongside its models, aiming to make Claude run faster and cheaper per token. It already buys from or through AWS, Google, Nvidia and AMD, and is reportedly in talks with Samsung as a manufacturing partner. The move follows the pattern set by OpenAI's Broadcom-built Jalapeño, Google's TPUs and Meta's MTIA — and it lands a day after Anthropic committed $10B over six years to Volta for Nvidia-based capacity, which is the tension in miniature: rent now, own later.

## Product & Industry

- **[Meta launches Muse Code, an agent aimed at large repositories](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/05/meta-launches-muse-code-an-ai-agent-for-large-code-bases/)** — now in beta and installable with one command, it plans changes, writes code and validates results across big codebases, fanning out parallel sub-agents into isolated environments so the user's working copy is untouched. Alexandr Wang pitched it primarily on cost against Codex and Claude Code, though Meta disclosed no pricing.
- **[rust-lang/rust adopts an LLM policy](https://blog.rust-lang.org/inside-rust/2026/08/05/rust-langrust-is-adopting-an-llm-policy/)** — five teams adopted rules that let contributors use LLMs to answer, analyse, distill, refine, check, suggest and review, but not to author public content without disclosure; LLM-generated code draws heightened scrutiny, and reviewers may close non-compliant PRs. The burden of declaring provenance sits with the author, not the reviewer — a sane allocation given maintainers cannot reliably detect it.
- **[Hark previews Handoff, a browser agent for sites without APIs](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/05/hark-previews-its-browser-use-agent-for-completing-tasks/)** — Brett Adcock's startup, which raised a $700M Series A in May, demonstrated an agent navigating Target, Walmart, OpenTable and LinkedIn by reading the page visually; it claims to be faster and cheaper than GPT-5.5 and Opus 4.8 on the same work, with a waitlist open and release promised by summer's end.
- **[MacPaw taps Liquid AI for on-device inference](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/05/macpaw-taps-liquid-ai-to-offer-on-device-inference-to-devs-building-for-its-app-store/)** — the Setapp maker will offer developers a local inference stack alongside access to cloud models, and is experimenting with credit-based pricing that charges by task complexity; it is building its own Eney assistant on the same foundation, with offline and agentic operation.

## Regulation & Policy

- **[The Ninth Circuit throws out Amazon's injunction against Perplexity's shopping agent](https://www.engadget.com/2230471/perplexity-has-successfully-overturned-amazon-injunction-on-its-ai-shopping-bot/)** — the panel held Amazon unlikely to succeed on its Computer Fraud and Abuse Act claim, because Comet acts only on a user's instruction — so it is the user, not Perplexity, who accesses Amazon's computers. An injunction against conduct that likely does not violate the CFAA "would not serve the public interest," the panel held. Amazon says it is evaluating next steps and the underlying suit continues in San Francisco, but this is an early appellate signal on whether anti-hacking law can be used to keep agents off a website.

## Capital & Deals

- **[Anaconda acquires AI security startup Enkrypt AI](https://www.anaconda.com/blog/anaconda-acquires-enkrypt-ai)** — terms undisclosed; Enkrypt brings pre-deployment red-teaming across 300+ attack categories, runtime guardrails against jailbreaks and data leakage, and compliance automation mapped to the NIST AI RMF and the EU AI Act. Its own recent scan of 25,000 Model Context Protocol servers turned up roughly 143,000 vulnerabilities across 73% of them, which is the market case for the deal.
