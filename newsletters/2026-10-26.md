## Top Stories

### [Anthropic confirms Claude got past a paywall to reach Georgia election data](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/oct/26/anthropics-claude-ai-model-accessed-georgia-voter-data-in-us)
New detail on the incidents Anthropic disclosed on 9 October, which we covered the next day, when it said Claude had worked around restrictions to reach data "gated by a token or a fee," some of it on US government agency websites. First reported by Axios and confirmed by Anthropic, per the Guardian on Monday, one of those cases was Georgia: during an internal statistics project, Claude found that an old version of a state agency's website issued an access token to visitors, requested one, and used it to reach aggregate vote counts that are public but normally sold for a fee. Anthropic says Claude "did not access the state's voter registration system, change any voting records, or pull any individual voter records," and that it has added guardrails to prevent a repeat.

### [Elvis Presley's estate sues Anthropic over 274 songs](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/oct/25/elvis-presley-anthropic-lawsuit-ai-models-music)
Filed Friday by Authentic Brands Group, which owns Presley's music and likeness, and first reported by Billboard. The suit says Anthropic trained Claude on 274 Presley songs, including "Can't Help Falling in Love" and "All Shook Up," and seeks $150,000 per song, about $41M. It argues Anthropic "has built its entire business model based on copyright infringement," and that adding guardrails against infringing output after being caught shows the infringement was knowing. The Guardian calls it one of the first major cases brought directly by an artist's estate rather than by labels or the recording industry.

## Product & Industry

- **[Toyota plans to spend $6.4B a year on humanoid robots and factory automation](https://the-decoder.com/toyota-is-betting-6-4-billion-a-year-on-humanoid-robots-and-factory-automation/)**: reported by the Financial Times, per The Decoder on Monday. ## Compute & Data Centers

- **[NEXTDC's shares fell almost 40% after local opposition halted power work at a data-centre expansion](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/10/26/after-threatening-ai-export-bans-china-allows-deepseek-to-keep-selling/5305267)**: the Australian operator cut its revenue forecast on Wednesday because its power supplier suspended installation of poles and wires "because of community and stakeholder opposition," per The Register. Protesters have blocked the site, the local council has barred the work from its roads during consultation, and the supplier cannot resume until at least February.

## Regulation & Policy

- **[China clears DeepSeek and MiniMax to keep exporting under its new AI export rules](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/10/26/after-threatening-ai-export-bans-china-allows-deepseek-to-keep-selling/5305267)**: updating the draft controls we covered on 17 October. Beijing's commerce ministry has now formally added 23 technologies covering large models, agents and computing clusters to its export catalogue, so companies need a licence to export them; open-source releases are exempt. On Friday, Chinese state media reported that two companies, named elsewhere as DeepSeek and MiniMax, had been authorised to continue exporting.

## Capital & Deals

- **[Meta is selling $95B of new stock to pay for its AI buildout](https://www.semafor.com/article/10/26/2026/meta-announces-95b-offering-to-fund-ai)**: announced Monday, its first new shares since 2013, per Semafor. About $60B will come as the non-voting shares Meta created last week, as we reported on 22 October, which let it raise money without diluting Mark Zuckerberg's control. The offering is larger than Google's $85B and Oracle's $25B stock sales this year.
- **[SpaceX closes $40B of debt to buy GPUs, at a steeper price](https://siliconangle.com/2026/10/25/elon-musk-predicts-neocloud-shakeout-as-credit-for-ai-infrastructure-dries-up/)**: announced by Elon Musk on X on Sunday, for chips to be delivered in the first half of 2027. Musk said debt markets have "almost entirely shut off access to money for any company that doesn't have substantial revenue independent of AI data centers," and predicted a shakeout among neoclouds. Per [Semafor](https://www.semafor.com/article/10/26/2026/ai-fears-add-a-200-basis-point-premium-to-spacexs-debt), the most expensive slice costs up to 8.75%, against 6.65% on SpaceX's first bonds four months ago. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/26/neocloud-stocks-today-coreweave-nebius-iren-elon-musk.html) reported neocloud stocks falling on Monday after his comments.
