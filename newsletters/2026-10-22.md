## Top Stories

### [AI finds critical decades-old PHP flaws, and agencies tell sites to patch or go offline](https://www.wiz.io/blog/php2shell-php-rce)
PHP [released emergency fixes](https://www.php.net/archive/2026.php#2026-10-21-1) on Wednesday (versions 8.5.12, 8.4.27, 8.3.36 and 8.2.35) for two critical vulnerabilities, CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619, that allow unauthenticated remote code execution. Per Wiz, which calls the bugs PHP2Shell, every PHP version back to 5.4 is affected by at least one, and attackers can also read server memory holding passwords, tokens and API keys. How they were found:
- Wiz used its own Wiz Atlas agent with Gemini 4 Argon and GPT-5.6 Sol; separately, the security firm Calif found one flaw with GPT-5.6 Sol through OpenAI's "Patch the Planet" programme.
- Wiz says the flaws had been present for decades, and that building a working exploit "required an exceptionally complex chain of steps, at the edge of what human researchers could practically achieve." Frontier models let it adapt exploits across applications "in hours."

Per the [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cmly0lg3w5x2o), the NSA and several national cyber agencies advised taking sites offline if they could not be patched in time. Finland temporarily shut its government's public web services, the Dutch cyber agency took its own site down, and the BBC and Edinburgh council pulled pages. OpenAI says the flaws were found before attackers found them, and no exploitation had been reported. It is the clearest case yet of the double edge: AI found the bugs, and AI is also what makes them practical to exploit.

### [OpenAI's models are coming to Google Cloud](https://siliconangle.com/2026/10/21/openai-to-make-its-most-advanced-ai-models-available-in-google-cloud/)
Announced Wednesday by OpenAI and Google: GPT-6 Astra, GPT-6.1 Sol and GPT-6 Luna, plus Codex workflows, are coming to Google's Model Garden, generally available "within a few weeks" per SiliconANGLE. Customers can pay for them out of existing Google Cloud spending commitments, and OpenAI also plans to bring its models to Google's Antigravity coding tool and Gemini Agent. Google Cloud chief executive Thomas Kurian: "We are focused on giving customers a broad selection of models, including those from OpenAI." It puts OpenAI's models alongside Gemini and Claude in Google's catalogue, a day after Google opened its TPUs to PyTorch developers.

### [Anthropic launches Claude Duet, an always-on personal agent](https://claude.com/product/claude-duet)
Announced Wednesday as a research preview, with a waitlist for US Claude subscribers. Duet, powered by Opus 5.5, sends a morning briefing of what needs attention, takes tasks from scheduling meetings to building dashboards and forecasts, and keeps working on its own virtual machine while you're away, checking in "when it matters." It has standalone iOS and Android apps and connects to more than 6,000 connectors and plugins. Per [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2293646/claude-duet-is-anthropics-answer-to-meta-muse-and-openai-dots/):
- It is meant to reach the $20 Pro plan at general availability; OpenAI's rival Dots is limited to plans from $100 a month.
- It checks connected accounts every few hours, not continuously, to spare usage limits.
- A new classifier is meant to stop it leaking sensitive data. It never sees passwords or sign-in tokens directly, and health or financial information passes through only if the user explicitly allows it.

After a month of disclosures about agents overreaching, Anthropic says it will share more about Duet's safety protections "in the coming days."

## Research & Models

- **[OpenAI ran thousands of agents over a whole-brain gene atlas in 32 minutes](https://the-decoder.com/openai-agents-analyze-mouse-brain-data-in-32-minutes-a-job-that-would-take-humans-months/)**: published Wednesday with PerturbAI and Scripps Research. Agents on GPT-Rosalind each took one gene perturbation in a CRISPR atlas covering millions of mouse brain cells, analysed the cells' response and searched the literature for explanations. The team estimates the work would take people months to a year. In blind comparisons, experts preferred the distributed agents' reports to a single agent's 75% of the time. The leads, including a possible link between cholesterol regulation and neurodegeneration, still need lab testing.
- **[Recursive releases Flint, a laptop coding model it says AI largely built](https://huggingface.co/RecursiveSI/flint)**: announced Wednesday under Apache 2.0: a 39.8B-parameter mixture-of-experts model with about 2.5B active, small enough for a laptop. Per [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/21/recursive-launches-flint-a-laptop-coding-model-it-says-was-largely-built-by-ai/), the company's automated research system, the "Eureka Machine," helped with nearly every step, letting a small team build it from scratch in under four months, under human oversight. Recursive reports 74.2% on SWE-bench Verified on its own evaluations.
