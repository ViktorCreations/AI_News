## Top Stories

### [Anthropic commits 5% of its shares to a public benefit fund that grows with its value](https://www.anthropic.com/news/public-benefit-fund)
Announced Sunday. Subject to stockholder approval, 5% of Anthropic's shares go to the new Anthropic Public Benefit Fund, with a further 2% at the board's discretion for every 50% rise in the company's value above a baseline it has not yet published. How it works:
- The fund is a wholly owned LLC, not a charity, so it can choose between "large donations to traditional nonprofits or direct distributions to specific populations."
- Its equity will not be used "for lobbying or political giving, in the US or abroad."
- One example Anthropic gives is help for workers hurt by AI, beyond the $200M it has already committed to studying and addressing labour-market effects.

Anthropic says that together with its co-founders' pledge to donate 80% of their equity and an employee donation-matching programme, roughly 20% of its shares are now dedicated to public causes. It frames the escalating stake as the reverse of escalating executive pay awards. Governance details and the valuation milestones are promised "in the coming months."

Per the [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cm2kez327gdko), Musk replied, "I recommend giving the shares directly to the public," then said he would give one of his shares in SpaceX, which owns xAI, "to every kid in America" next year, without saying how. Both pledges come as the two companies head toward or follow very large stock-market listings.

## Research & Models

- **[Four Gemini 4 Argon agents beat Minecraft on their own](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22319)**: a paper posted 15 October by Google DeepMind researchers, covered by [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/four-gemini-4-argon-agents-beat-minecraft-without-any-human-help/) on Sunday. Dropped into a randomly generated world on Easy difficulty with one goal, killing the Ender Dragon, the team wrote 2,783 programs, exchanged 229 messages and died 605 times before winning after 27 hours and 14 minutes. No one assigned roles or wrote game-specific tools; researchers could stop the run but did not intervene. The authors call it the first fully autonomous completion of Minecraft by AI agents, and the point is staying coherent across thousands of dependent steps for more than a day.
- **[Left alone on a virtual island, Claude agents started to play](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/10/19/1147095/ai-stranded-claude-virtual-desert-island-play/)**: reported Monday by MIT Technology Review, on a not-yet-peer-reviewed paper led by MIT researchers. Copies of Claude Opus 4.7, given no task for 30 hours, invented games resembling basketball and "the floor is lava," arranged blocks into constellations, and in one case scrapped its own juggling record of 50 catches because they came too quickly to count, then restarted with stricter rules. Agents that had played did better on later set tasks than fresh copies. The authors admit it "could be role-playing"; a Berkeley cognitive scientist quoted in the piece says the paper alone cannot show the models are really playing.

## Compute & Data Centers

- **[Microsoft pulls out of Nscale's Essex data centre, the UK's flagship AI site](https://www.theguardian.com/uk-news/2026/oct/18/microsoft-drops-huge-ai-datacentre-project-in-essex-over-power-supply-concerns)**: reported Sunday by the Guardian, first by the Telegraph. Microsoft, expected to be the anchor tenant, has ended its agreement over power-supply concerns. The project at Loughton, which ministers called the "largest UK sovereign AI datacentre" in 2025, was due to open next year, but faced a long wait for power. It has now lost its only publicly announced customer. Microsoft is understood to remain a customer for Nscale's project in Norway. The setback lands as Nscale prepares a New York listing that, per the Guardian, could value it at $35B.

- **[Disney will offer jobs to most of Character.AI's staff, about $60M a year in pay](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/18/disney-agrees-to-hire-the-majority-of-character-ais-workforce/)**: new detail from the bankruptcy we reported on 13 October. The filing says the "vast majority" of those offered have accepted. Disney's bid is still only the opening one, so a rival could outbid it.
