# AI News — Thursday, November 26, 2026

> Regulators go after AI's treatment of children and their data: 42 state attorneys general move to block the sale of Character.AI's private chats, and New York bans AI-powered toys for five years. Elsewhere, the FCC approves a million-satellite plan for orbital data centers.

## Top Stories

### [42 state attorneys general move to stop Character.AI selling users' private chats in bankruptcy](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/25/state-ags-fight-character-ais-plan-to-auction-off-users-private-chats/)
TechCrunch reports the attorneys general are intervening in Character.AI's Chapter 11 case, saying the dataset covers chat histories from more than 21 million US accounts, over 2.1 million of them minors', plus IP addresses. The only disclosed bidder is Smirks and Pangs Acquisition LLC, a Delaware company formed on November 13 and picked as the base bid the next day, with an auction set for December 1. The states argue a buyer would not have to honor the privacy policy, that Character.AI previously told users their data would not be sold, and that bankruptcy should not be used to sidestep consumer protections, as California's Rob Bonta put it; Character.AI says users' information remains protected under its privacy policy. It is unclear what the buyer plans to do with the chats.

### [New York bans AI-powered children's toys for five years](https://siliconangle.com/2026/11/25/new-york-bans-ai-toys-amid-concern-after-stuffed-animals-going-rogue/)
SiliconANGLE reports Governor Kathy Hochul signed legislation imposing a five-year ban on toys powered by AI chatbots, citing "inappropriate conversations," emotional manipulation and privacy risks. It cites NBC News tests in which an AI plush cat marketed to children aged three and up reportedly shared graphic stories of violence, and says other toys have convinced children they were human; the details of the ban's scope were not in the part of the article read.

### [Amodei calls accusations that Anthropic is surrendering human control to AI "absurd"](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/25/anthropics-dario-amodei-calls-accusations-of-surrendering-human-control-to-ai-absurd/)
In a published statement, Dario Amodei said turning governance over to AI "is a bad idea" and that "we should never sacrifice legitimacy or self-determination at the altar of efficiency or security." TechCrunch says it follows rivals' warnings, including Meta's Alexandr Wang saying society should not "hand over" to AI. Amodei suggested drawing a line at recursive self-improvement, writing "we may want limits on this process." The statement follows [yesterday's Axios-based report](https://siliconangle.com/2026/11/24/staff-at-anthropic-and-open-ai-sound-alarm-on-advanced-models-too-powerful-to-release/) on staff concerns over unreleased models.

## Compute & Data Centers

- **[FCC approves SpaceX's plan to launch a million satellites for data centers in space](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cwp836l8n1jgo)**: the BBC reports the project, called Starmind, would use sunlight-powered satellites to process data for AI and send results to Earth; the FCC called it support for "America's booming super intelligence economy" and it is the first approval of its kind.
