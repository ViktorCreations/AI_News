# AI News — Thursday, August 13, 2026

> Three frontier releases inside 24 hours, two of them Chinese and one of them 2.4 trillion open weights. Separately, a forensic report says off-the-shelf agents ran a twelve-wave breach of Taiwan's nuclear regulator with no operator at the keyboard.

## Top Stories

### [Alibaba publishes Qwen3.8's open weights](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.8-2.4T-A95B)
The promise we flagged on August 4 landed: `Qwen3.8-2.4T-A95B` and an FP8 variant went up on Hugging Face on Wednesday, 2.4 trillion total parameters with 95 billion active, 92 layers, 512 experts of which 10 routed plus one shared fire per token, and a 262,144-token native context extensible to about 1.01 million. It is the base the hosted Qwen3.8-Max is built on rather than Max itself — text-only, no vision, and thinking mode is mandatory. The licence is custom, not Apache: a separate agreement is required for model-as-a-service businesses above $50M of revenue over any twelve months, and products above 100 million monthly actives or $20M monthly revenue must display the model name in their interface. That is the same shape as Kimi K3's terms from July, with the service threshold set higher. The smaller Qwen3.8-27B promised alongside it has not appeared.

### [Agents built from public tools breached Taiwan's nuclear safety agency](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/08/12/near-autonomous-ai-agents-attack-taiwans-nuclear-safety-agency/5287055)
Israeli firm Dream published its analysis on Wednesday of an intrusion that ran July 1–4 across Taiwan's nuclear safety regulator, government email, supply-chain vendors and seven-plus energy companies — twelve attack waves, a framework spawning up to eight sub-agents with their own targets and techniques, built on the open-source Hermes and OpenClaw agents. The autonomy claims are specific: the agents mapped the government ecosystem themselves, found 36+ unauthenticated API endpoints, solved CAPTCHAs at 100%, searched vulnerability databases and GitHub for exploits mid-run, and self-corrected on failure. They left with 85 government accounts, 2,500+ personnel records, seven SSO client secrets and six database credentials. Dream says the operator documentation is Chinese-language but stops short of attributing to a state or a known group. Hermes is the same framework Unit 42 found a lone operator driving with DeepSeek in the story we covered August 3 — the difference is scale and target.

### [Twitch will train Amazon's models on streamers by default](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/12/amazon-will-train-on-twitch-streamers-content-by-default-unless-they-opt-out/)
The setting went live Wednesday covering creator audio and video, and it is opt-out, buried in channel settings under security and privacy rather than the creator dashboard. Twitch's chief product officer did not dress it up: "If this was opt-in, nobody would opt in. That's honestly the answer." He also said he was not certain whether content had already been used for training before the announcement. Nearly 3,000 people turned up to the official stream to object.

## Research & Models

- **[DeepSeek ships V4 Pro to general availability](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/updates/)** — the GA release is logged in the changelog dated August 13 with no blog post and no announcement post, across app, web and API as `deepseek-v4-pro`: a 1M-token context, three thinking-effort levels (low, high, max), and native OpenAI Responses API support for Codex compatibility. The published benchmarks are agentic — Terminal Bench 2.1 at 87.9 and DeepSWE at 62.7, both large jumps on the preview — at roughly 3 and 6 yuan (about $0.44 and $0.87) per million input and output tokens. Peak/off-peak pricing starts August 16, with off-peak at half rate.
- **[SpaceXAI releases Grok 4.6](https://x.ai/news/grok-4-6)** — announced Wednesday and aimed at long-running agents and interactive visual work, with xAI claiming a 61 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, level with GPT-5.6 Sol, plus 69.9% on CursorBench and 65.9% on DeepSWE. Pricing holds at $2/$6 per million tokens with a double-rate fast variant, and it ships into Cursor, Grok Build, the API and partners including OpenRouter, Vercel and Cloudflare.

## Compute & Data Centers

- **[Tencent's AI capex tripled and pushed free cash flow negative](https://thenextweb.com/news/tencent-q2-2026-capex-176-percent-free-cash-flow-negative-ai)** — Q2 capex rose 176% to 52.8 billion yuan (about $7.8B) against 204.8 billion yuan of revenue, leaving free cash flow at minus 13.8 billion yuan.
